From data to decision

A roadmap for closing the loop.

The current release simulates scenarios. Automatic collection, experiment registration and recalibration below are future work.

01 Observe A future evidence collector captures commercial behaviour — calls, CRM activity, offers, objections, follow-ups and the outcome that resolved.
02 Learn Actions get connected to resolved outcomes, with the economics attached: what was done, to whom, at what stage, and what it was worth.
03 Simulate Fate evaluates an alternative decision against the current policy and returns a distribution of conversion, revenue and gross profit.
04 Validate The estimate is recorded before the change ships. When the outcome resolves, prediction and reality are compared on the same cohort.
05 Improve Recalibrate. Where the estimate was wrong, the interval widens; where it held, the system earns the right to speak more confidently.

Assumptions behind this estimate

  • Relative lift is a blended prior, not a measured treatment effect.

What this is. A transparent scenario calculation: your cohort, an explicit offer definition, deterministic revenue and margin arithmetic, and a seeded outcome distribution. Same inputs, same result, every time.

What it is not. Not a prediction, not a proven causal effect, and not evidence that the offer caused the lift. The interval propagates uncertainty in the baseline rate and the lift assumption — not in deal value, timing, seasonality, seller effects or capacity.

Positioning

AI can write the email.The harder question is whether you should change the offer at all.

Most AI tools help execute work faster. Fate is built for the decision above the work — the one that determines whether the work was worth doing. That requires a different architecture, and a strict division of labour. The live connectors described below are an architectural roadmap; this release accepts CSV and synthetic data.

Language models interpret.

Read transcripts, objections and interactions. Compile a scenario written in plain English into a structured, typed intervention.

NeverInvent a conversion rate, an effect size, or any financial input.

Statistical models estimate.

Produce probabilities and effect sizes from resolved outcomes, with intervals, coverage and the segments they actually support.

NeverHide unsupported extrapolation behind a confident point estimate.

Deterministic software calculates.

Revenue, margin, discount, financing cost and gross profit — versioned, reproducible, and auditable line by line.

NeverCall a language model to do arithmetic.

Reality validates.

The decision ships, the outcome resolves, and the estimate is scored against what actually happened on that exact cohort.

NeverTreat a fluent simulation as evidence that it was right.

The closed loop

Every decision you make
becomes evidence.

A prediction is registered before the change ships. The outcome resolves against the same cohort. The gap between them is the only thing that makes the next estimate better.

Illustrative ledger. A missed prediction is kept and scored — it is the most valuable row in the table.

The moat is not the number of AI agents, or the number of synthetic simulations. Running more simulated worlds reduces sampling noise; it does not remove bias, and it cannot establish that a change caused an outcome.

What compounds instead is a growing dataset connecting four things that almost nobody stores together: what we knew → what we predicted → what action was taken → what actually happened. That is the asset, and it can only be built one resolved decision at a time.

Uncertainty

Sometimes the right answer
is: we don't know yet.

A system that expresses precision where the evidence is thin is worse than no system. Fate is built to say so — and saying so is the feature, not the limitation.

Abstain

< 10 opportunities, or < 2 resolved wins

Fate refuses to produce an estimate and tells you what evidence is missing. There is no number here worth showing.

Exploratory

Thin cohort, or evidence leaning on priors

The arithmetic runs, but the result is for shaping a question — not allocating capital. The recommended next step is to design a controlled test.

Directional

Sufficient resolved outcomes in the cohort

Strong enough to compare options, size a test and rank interventions. Still an estimate under a stated assumption, not a measured effect.

Without evidence from a controlled experiment, Fate caps its own confidence score. High confidence is something the system has to earn from reality, not assert.

  • Expose every assumption behind the number
  • Show ranges instead of false precision
  • Report evidence strength and where it came from
  • Separate company, industry and synthetic evidence
  • Flag populations the evidence doesn't support
  • Abstain when the evidence can't carry a decision
  • Recommend an experiment when one is needed
  • Export complete result snapshots for inspection

Where this goes

Each step has to be earned
before we claim the next one.

The ambition is a decision system. The discipline is refusing to describe it as one before the evidence says so.

STAGE 01 Scenario “What if?” Today
STAGE 02 Predict “What is likely?” Calibrated models
STAGE 03 Understand “What actually changes the outcome?” Causal estimation
STAGE 04 Decide “Which action produces the best expected business result?” Policy comparison
STAGE 05 Learn “Did reality match the prediction?” Continuous

Stages two through five are the roadmap, not the product. We will say when each one arrives, and we will show the evaluation that justified it.
